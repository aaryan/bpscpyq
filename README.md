# bpscpyq
**BPSC PYQ and Notes** (bpscpyq.com) is a specialized, open-access preparation portal built to streamline and elevate preliminary exam readiness for Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC) Civil Services aspirants.

Preparing for the BPSC Combined Competitive Examination (CCE) has traditionally required juggling heavy reference books, deciphering disputed unofficial answer keys, and scrolling through disorganized question PDFs. bpscpyq.com solves this by organizing decades of past papers and syllabus theory into an interactive, mobile-optimized, and zero-distraction study environment.

---

### Our Core Mission

The platform is designed around two cognitive learning principles: **Active Recall** and **Targeted Question Practice**.

Rather than relying on passive reading, bpscpyq.com provides candidates with direct access to categorized past-year questions and bite-sized revision notes that test immediate memory retrieval. The goal is to eliminate factual ambiguity, master past examination trends from the 38th through the 71st BPSC Prelims, and build the speed and precision required on exam day.

---

### What We Offer

* **3,058+ Verified Chapterwise PYQs**: Comprehensive coverage of previous year questions spanning from 1992 to 2025. Every question is mapped to its specific syllabus topic, checked against official answer keys, and paired with authentic historical, constitutional, and scientific explanations.
* **75 Chapters of Active Recall Flashnotes**: High-yield theory summaries mapped to NCERTs and standard reference texts. Decks feature dual **Read Mode** (complete notes with emphasized key terms) and **Recall Mode** (interactive cloze deletions and true/false drills) across core disciplines:
* **History**: Comprehensive modules for Ancient India, Medieval India, and Modern India (1498–1947).
* **Bihar Special**: Specialized state-specific modules covering Bihar’s freedom struggle, physical geography, rivers, agro-climatic zones, minerals, and governance.
* **Indian Polity & Constitution**: In-depth coverage of constitutional evolution, Preamble, Fundamental Rights, DPSP, Union Executive, Parliament, Judiciary, and Statutory Bodies.
* **General Science**: Core revision units across Biology, Chemistry, and Physics.
* **Geography**: Indian physical features, drainage basins, climate classification, and world geography essentials.


* **Dual Exam & Practice Simulation**:
* **Practice Mode**: Immediate answer revelation, detailed explanatory notes, and stopwatch tracking per question.
* **Exam Mode**: Simulates actual BPSC test conditions with countdown timers (48 seconds per question), a question status palette, and standard negative marking (1/3rd deduction) to benchmark accuracy.


* **Targeted Filtering & Progress Tracking**: Filter questions by unattempted status, previously missed questions, starred bookmarks, or specific exam years. All progress is preserved directly in the user’s browser for private, friction-free continuity.
