123 previous-year questions from the Medieval History section of the BPSC Prelims General Studies chapterwise solved papers, covering the Delhi Sultanate through the decline of the Mughals. Every answer and explanation is the book’s own. Four questions carry a disputed or cancelled official key and are flagged where they appear.

The chip beside the star shows how long you have been on the current question. It stops the moment you answer or reveal, so the stored figure is time taken to attempt, not time spent reading the explanation. It pauses when the tab is in the background.

Mock tests draw a random set from whichever pool you choose, run against a countdown, and score with negative marking. BPSC deducts one-third of a mark for a wrong answer and allows 150 questions in 120 minutes, which is the 48-second pace used throughout.
